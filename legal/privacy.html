Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. About this page

This website is a promotional page for the free-to-play game Ragnarok: The New World. It does not ask you to create an account, log in or fill in a form, and we do not ask you for personal data such as your name or email address.

2. What we may collect

Like most websites, the page and the services it relies on may receive technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser type, device, language, the page you came from, the pages you view and the buttons you click. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, and is used to run the page, measure traffic and measure advertising results.

3. When you press "Play Free on Steam"

The button sends you to the game page on Steam. After you reach Steam, Valve (Steam) and the game's publisher handle your data under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your Steam or game account details.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts, used to display the page text, may receive your IP address.
  • A country lookup service (Cloudflare, with api.country.is as a backup), called once when you open the page to choose its language, so that visitors connecting from Thailand see it in Thai. It receives your IP address, as any website you connect to does. We do not store it; only the country code is kept in your browser for the visit.
  • Advertising and analytics tools used to run campaigns may set their own cookies.
  • Steam (Valve) and the game's publisher, after you leave this page.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details and how to control cookies.

6. Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or stop using the page at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) or the EU GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its use. Because we do not hold an account about you, requests mostly concern the third-party services above.

7. Children

This page is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
